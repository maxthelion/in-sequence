as a builder-facing prep package. The accepted first slice is bounded around
kit-track matrix compression, drum-part row/name visibility, kit-matrix
title/pattern waste reduction, and drum-part sound-source affordance.

## Explicit Scope Boundary

In scope for PM prep:

- Kit-track matrix compression and removal of obvious wasted space in the kit
  track surface.
- Drum part naming/row visibility and compact kit/part grammar needed for the
  same first build pass.
- Drum-part sound-source affordance, including empty-vs-none language and
  load-source/load-AU affordance only as UX contract.

Out of scope:

- Slicer or general track-header compression outside the kit-track comparison
  needed to understand G6.
- Mixer strip, send, routing, or FX surface redesign.
- AU runtime safety, preset application/removal while playing, AU hosting
  lifecycle, or human-present AU validation.
- Scenes IA or scene perform behavior.
- Track Perform mini-cell direct-selection work already landed separately.
- Product-code edits, build-loop creation/promotion, merge/push/delete
  operations, visual automation, and bug resolution status changes.

## Readiness

product-owner lock is needed for the first pass, and no additional PM

- avoid churn for acceptance criteria already satisfied on main;

Product-owner attention is only needed if implementation uncovers a new choice
such as shared whole-kit AU versus per-part own-AU behavior, or if satisfying
the surface contract would require AU runtime lifecycle validation.
