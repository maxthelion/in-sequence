## Overview

These pages capture external devices, sibling projects, and earlier prototypes that inform sequencer-ai. They are reference material rather than current application architecture.

## Reference Devices

- [[octatrack-reference]] — parts, trigs, parameter locks, scenes, conditional trigs, and arranger concepts.
- [[cirklon-reference]] — instrument definitions, P3/CK patterns, aux rows, scenes, and song mode.
- [[polyend-play-reference]] — work steps, per-track independence, variations, chance/action, fill, and perform mode.

## Sibling And Source Projects

- [[glaypen-reference]] — generative MIDI, pitch history, generator output capture, and clip freezing.
- [[progressive-reference]] — chord progressions, voice leading, chord performance, and chord stream ideas.
- [[sequencerbox-domain-model]] — Song, Pattern, Scene, Track, Clip, Trigger, and Sound model structure.
- [[phatcontroller]] — browser MIDI step sequencing and parameter automation over Web MIDI.
