# --- CoreMIDI callback publication (2026-06-12 lane finding 3) ---
# Three test-helper packet recorders are constructed on the test main
# thread, then handed to MIDIClient.createVirtualInput; their first
# `append` runs on CoreMIDI's receive thread (CADeprecated::CAPThread).
# TSan flags init-vs-first-append as unordered, but the happens-before
# edge is MIDIDestinationCreate's registration round trip through
# MIDIServer (cross-process), which TSan cannot model: the receive thread
# cannot invoke the handler before registration completes, and
# registration starts strictly after init returns. All post-init access
# in all three helpers is NSLock-guarded (verified 2026-06-12).
race:IntegrationMIDIPacketRecorder
race:LockedPacketStore
race:LockedMIDIPacketStore
