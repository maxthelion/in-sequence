Story 4 — Perform-mode save-back (annotation only)
Why no wireframe: The existing-state report (§4a) shows that track-matrix perform mode writes edits immediately into the canonical phrase model — there is no staging layer. The desired UX (seed from phrase, save-back or discard) requires a new per-phrase overlay architecture analogous to MasterBusPerformanceOverlayState in the scenes layer. Until that architecture is designed, a perform save-back wireframe would depict a flow with no backing mechanism and could mislead the build loop.

Recommended next step: Define the staging architecture in spec.md before prototyping the save-back UI. At that point the wireframe can show: (a) perform-mode action bar with "Save to Phrase" and "Discard" buttons; (b) visual diff between staged edits and phrase baseline; (c) conflict behaviour if the original phrase was modified during the session.
