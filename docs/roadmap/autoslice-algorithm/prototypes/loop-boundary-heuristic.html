Autoslice — Sandbox 3: Ranked Loop Boundary Heuristic + Role Classification

This sandbox explores the full loop-start/end suggestion pipeline. Given a transient list (optionally with role hints: kick, snare, hi-hat), it generates ranked loop-start candidates by scoring grid alignment — first without role weighting, then with it. Demonstrates Story 3 (slightly-too-long), Story 4 (ranked candidates), and Story 5 (role classification as a weighting factor).
