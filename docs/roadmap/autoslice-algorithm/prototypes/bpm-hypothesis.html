Autoslice — Sandbox 1: BPM Hypothesis from Sample Duration

Given a sample duration in seconds, this sandbox computes ranked BPM + bar-count candidates by inverting the loop-duration formula. For each (bars, BPM) pair, it shows how close the predicted duration is to the actual duration and a fit score. This covers User Story 1 (BPM hypothesis) and Story 4 (multiple ranked suggestions).
