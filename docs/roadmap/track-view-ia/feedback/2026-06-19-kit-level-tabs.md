# Kit-Level Tabs: FX, Macros, Mixer (+ Kit Perform)

Raw product-owner clarification:

> we also need some kit tabs for fx, kit macros and possibly other things (kit
> perform button).

Interpretation for build:

- The kit altitude gets its own tab bar paralleling the track tabs:
  **Matrix · FX · Macros · Mixer** (History is reached via Capture — see the
  patterns item).
- **Kit FX** = the insert chain on the kit's own bus (FX across the whole kit).
  Distinct from per-part FX (reached by expanding a row).
- **Kit Macros** = M1–M8 that drive a parameter across **all parts at once**
  (e.g. cutoff on every part) or the bus FX.
- **Kit Mixer** = the kit bus: output (→ Master), Send A/B, and per-part levels.
- FX and macros therefore exist at **two scopes**: kit-bus and per-part.
- A **Kit Perform** header button opens the scoped perform surface for the whole
  kit.
