# Drum-Part Filter Stays In The Mini Sampler

Raw product-owner clarification:

> the sound for drum parts is a combination of the sampler plus a filter ...
> for now, let's keep filter as part of the mini sampler for drum kits rather
> than fully separating.

Interpretation for build:

- A drum part's sound is a **sampler + filter voice**. For this pass the filter
  stays **inside the mini sampler** (part of the Sound tab), not the FX chain.
- The filter cleanup (radial controls, more types, no wet/dry, curve viz) applies
  to that in-sampler filter.
- Fully separating the per-part filter into the FX chain is **deferred**.
