# Track View IA — User Stories

1. As a producer, I want a track's editor split into clear tabs (Steps/Clip,
   Sound, FX, Macros, Mixer) so each concern has its own place. (AC1–AC3)
2. As a producer, I want a real per-track FX chain I can reorder by dragging,
   bypass, and remove inline, added with a "+ FX" button. (AC4–AC5)
3. As a producer, I want a filter that looks good and is expressive — radial
   controls, more types, a curve I can see, and no wet/dry clutter. (AC6–AC7)
4. As a producer, I want sensible macro defaults on a new drum part so it's
   playable immediately, but still fully editable. (AC8)
5. As a beatmaker, I want the kit grid capped at 16 steps with a bar pager and
   part names on the left, so I can see more parts at once. (AC9–AC10)
6. As a beatmaker, I want a new kit to go to its own bus so I can FX the whole
   kit at once. (AC11, AC23)
7. As a performer, I want the kit's patterns always visible and a one-press
   Capture and Perform, so I can assign and play without digging. (AC12–AC14)
8. As a performer, I want to capture the whole kit's recent history as one clip
   set and scrub back to the moment I liked. (AC15–AC16)
9. As a beatmaker, I want kit patterns to switch together when linked, but to
   still tweak one part without ceremony — diverging just unlinks that part.
   (AC17–AC20)
10. As a sound designer, I want to expand a kit part inline to its Sound/FX/Macros
    /Mixer (and switch a part between clip and generator) without leaving the kit.
    (AC21)
11. As a performer, I want to drop into perform mode scoped to just one track or
    kit, reusing the perform UI I already know. (AC22)
