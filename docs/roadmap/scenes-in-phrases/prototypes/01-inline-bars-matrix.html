Why this variant exists

  • Keeps all authored scene data visible on the phrase row.
  • Makes per-bar movement scannable without opening another editor.
  • Tests whether taller rows are acceptable once multiple phrases are in view.

Known trade-off

  • Per-bar phrases consume vertical space fast.
  • Rows with static blends feel lighter than rows with bar automation.
  • The off-path scene library browser remains stubbed on purpose.
