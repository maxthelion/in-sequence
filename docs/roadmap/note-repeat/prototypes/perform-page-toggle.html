Scenario A — Idle

Story 1

Normal perform mode. All tracks are playing their sequenced content. The Note Repeat toggle is visible per-track in the action row but inactive (dashed border = proposed new UI).

New component: Perform Action Row
A 32px strip pinned above the track-card grid, visible only in perform mode. Each column aligns with its track card. Hosts [Fill] and [Repeat] per track. Does not affect which layer is selected.
Gap (existing-state report): Fill today is a phrase-layer boolean toggled by layer selection, not a runtime button. The [Fill] button shown here is the target behaviour, not what currently exists. Both Fill and Repeat need a live runtime override path.

Interaction budget

1 tap to engage Note Repeat on any track. Switch to Scenario B to see engaged state.

Click path

  • Tap [Repeat] on any track column → Scenario B (capture begins)
  • Tap [Fill] on any track column → fill activates for that track (stub)

Scenario B — Repeat Engaged (Kick)

Story 1 Story 2

Performer pressed and held [Repeat] on Kick at transport position step 9. The sequencer snapped to step 9's content and began looping it at the configured interval (1/16 here).

Visual language used:

  • Repeat button: amber/orange fill, pulsing — "hold active"
  • Track-index label: blue background — track is in repeat state
  • Track card: amber left border, amber step dot, "CAPTURED: step N" overlay
  • Step dot for captured step: amber + flash animation — this step is firing repeatedly
  • Other tracks: continue advancing normally (transport is not frozen)
Open (existing-state report): The transport position for non-repeating tracks advances while Kick is frozen on step 9. When Kick is released it must re-join at the current transport step, not at step 9. The engage/disengage API does not yet exist.

Click path

  • Tap [Repeat] on Kick (amber button) → releases → Scenario C
  • Other [Repeat] and [Fill] buttons: stubbed, show console message

Scenario C — Released (resuming)

Story 2

Performer released [Repeat] on Kick. The engine flushed pending note-offs for the captured step and re-joined normal sequence playback at the next step boundary (step 12 here — the transport had advanced to step 12 while Kick was frozen).

Key behaviour this scenario validates:

  • Kick's current-step indicator jumps to step 12, not back to step 9
  • No stuck notes: all pending note-offs were flushed on disengage
  • The Repeat button returns to its idle (dashed border) style immediately on release
  • Other tracks were unaffected throughout
Engine requirement (existing-state report): MidiOut.flushPendingNoteOffs(now:) and the audio-path equivalent must be called on disengage. The re-join step must be determined by the live transport position at the moment of release, not by the captured step index.

Click path

  • Tap [Repeat] on Kick (idle button) → Scenario B (re-engage)
Interaction budget: 1 tap to engage Note Repeat • 1 tap to release • Total: 2 taps for full engage/disengage round-trip. Budget met.
