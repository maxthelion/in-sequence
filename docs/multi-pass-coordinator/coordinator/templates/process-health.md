Last process health observer review:

## Purpose

Track whether the multi-pass loop is actually helping agents build, check, and
repair product work with low product-owner attention.

## Health Checklist

- [ ] Builders are regularly doing product-code work rather than only process
      bookkeeping
- [ ] Builder outputs are checked by relevant review/observer loops
- [ ] Review failures are routed into concrete rework
- [ ] Actor logs do not show repeated environment, permission, timeout, memory,
      or tooling failures
- [ ] Deterministic scripts provide enough cheap orientation for agents
- [ ] Inbox handoffs are clear and not duplicative
- [ ] Coordinator decisions lead to action or deliberate waiting, not churn
- [ ] Product-owner attention is protected from agent-detectable problems
